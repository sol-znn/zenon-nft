Threshold release

A way to build the Virtual Hologram and its Embedded Unlockable Content from Zenon's 2022 article. The content key is sealed once to the Sentinels, and only the NFT holder the ledger names can reveal the content.116

What it costs

    

    

      
Collusion
Any t Sentinels together can open everything sealed to their key. The bridge trusts its orchestrators the same way.13

      
Liveness
Fewer than t online and nobody opens. The holder wrap on the ledger still does.16

      
Privacy
The committee sees who opens which item, and when. It never sees the key or the content.

      
Not proxy re-encryption
A key holder still acts at every transfer, and single-hop schemes cannot follow a chain of holders.1516
